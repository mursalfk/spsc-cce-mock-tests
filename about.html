What you get

  • Three levels. Easy tests are pitched at the real paper, a little above it in places. Medium tests sit a clear step above it: finer facts, look-alike options, multi-step maths and reasoning. Hard tests are deliberately tougher than the real thing: close distractors, fine distinctions, and about one question in four in a statement, NOT or timeline form. Pick a level on the home page.
  • One question per screen, like the computer-based test. Previous, skip and mark for later all work, and a question list shows where you stand.
  • Instant feedback. Tap an answer and it tells you at once if it is right or wrong, then locks. An explanation is one tap away and entirely optional.
  • Exam rules. +1 for a right answer, −0.25 for a wrong one, 0 for a skipped one. An optional 200-minute countdown keeps you honest.
  • A proper review. After each test: your net score against the pass mark, a section breakdown, every question with its answer, and flashcards for what you missed or skipped.
  • A PDF report emailed to you every time you finish a test. The download button always works, even if email does not.
  • Keyboard friendly. 1 to 4 or A to D to answer, E for the explanation, the arrow keys to move, M to mark.
  • Five looks and four text sizes. Tap Aa at the top right of any page.
  • A full topic list. See every topic and subtopic in every test, with question counts, on the topics page.

A blind guess among four options is worth only about +0.06 on average, so guess once you can rule out at least one option.

Please read this

  • Unofficial. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Sindh Public Service Commission.
  • Original questions. They were written for this project. None are copied from past papers or books. Each question appears once in a level; the same fact may come up again in a different question.
  • Four options (A to D) are assumed. The exact layout of the 2026 paper is not confirmed.
  • Current Affairs is dated to about 7 November 2026. In the last week before the exam, re-check office-holders, results and anything with "currently" in the question.
  • With … questions, a few will be wrong or arguable. The Medium and Hard tests lean on finer facts, so check a surprising answer before trusting it. If you find one, please report it (see below).

Your data

  • Sign-in is by email and password, or with Google, through Firebase Authentication. Your name and email are kept there so the site can recognise you.
  • Results. Each finished test saves one record to your own account in Cloud Firestore: test number, score, right, wrong and skipped counts, section breakdown and time used. Security rules let only you read or write it. Your individual answers are not stored in the cloud.
  • Progress on a test in progress is kept in your browser so you can resume. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.
  • Email. When you finish, your browser builds the PDF and asks a small Google Apps Script service to send it. The service checks your sign-in and sends only to your own verified address, from the name "SPSC CCE Mock Test Practice (Unofficial)". Sending goes through a Gmail account with a daily limit shared by all users, so the download button is the reliable copy.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers of its own. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Want your account and results removed? Message the author through the portfolio link below.
