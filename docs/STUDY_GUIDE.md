# Using the three levels

- **Easy** is pitched at the paper, a little above it in places. Use it to learn the timing and to find your weak sections. Aim for 66 net and hold it.
- **Medium** is a clear step above the paper: finer facts, look-alike options, multi-step maths and reasoning, statement and sequence questions.
- **Hard** is deliberately above the paper: close distractors, fine distinctions, multi-step maths and reasoning, and about one question in four in a statement, NOT or timeline form.

A sensible rhythm for the last days: one full Medium or Hard test under the timer, then read the wrong answers and their notes before the next one. Skip when unsure. A wrong answer costs 0.25, a skipped one costs nothing.

Use the topics page (linked from About) to see which topics and subtopics each test covers and to spot gaps in your revision.

If a question looks wrong or arguable, report it with the level, test number and question number.
